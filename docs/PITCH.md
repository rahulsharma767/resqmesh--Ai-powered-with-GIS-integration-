# 60-Second Pitch — ResQMesh AI

**0–10 sec — Problem**
Emergency calls are messy, incomplete and time-sensitive. At the same time, roads, ambulances and hospital capacity keep changing.

**10–20 sec — AI**
ResQMesh AI turns the structured incident state into a real local ML risk assessment: risk score, priority, confidence and the learned factors driving the result.

**20–30 sec — Response**
That AI does not get to dispatch an ambulance by itself. Our deterministic ResQMesh engine verifies fleet capability, calculates the road route and checks hospital feasibility.

**30–40 sec — Change the city**
Now traffic increases and a road closes. The GIS engine detects route failure and calculates an alternate path.

**40–50 sec — Reassess**
The AI runs again against the changed operational state. We can see the risk and confidence update while the deterministic router revalidates the response.

**50–60 sec — Snapdragon + safety**
The model is packaged for ONNX Runtime with a Qualcomm QNN execution path when available, while the UI reports the real provider instead of faking NPU claims. AI recommends, deterministic systems verify, and humans remain in control.
